# Japanese particles explained

**Short answer:** Particles come **after** the word they mark and tell you its role in the sentence. **は (wa)** marks the topic ("as for X"). **が (ga)** marks the subject, often new or emphasised information. **を (o)** marks the direct object. **に (ni)** marks a destination, a point in time, or where something exists. **で (de)** marks where an action happens or the means used. **の (no)** links nouns ("X's Y"). **も (mo)** means "also" and replaces は, が or を.

| Particle | Main job | Example | Meaning |
|---|---|---|---|
| は (wa) | topic | 私**は**学生です。 | As for me, (I) am a student. |
| が (ga) | subject / new info | 猫**が**いる。 | There's a cat. |
| を (o) | direct object | 本**を**読む。 | (I) read a book. |
| に (ni) | destination, time, existence location | 東京**に**行く。 | (I) go to Tokyo. |
| で (de) | place of action, means | 図書館**で**勉強する。 | (I) study at the library. |
| の (no) | possession / linking nouns | 私**の**本 | my book |
| へ (e) | direction | 日本**へ**行く。 | (I) head to Japan. |
| と (to) | "and" (nouns), "with" | 友達**と**話す。 | (I) talk with a friend. |
| も (mo) | also | 私**も**学生です。 | I'm a student too. |
| から / まで | from / until | 9時**から**5時**まで** | from 9 to 5 |

## は vs が

This is the question learners ask most. Three working rules:

1. **は sets the topic; が picks out the subject.**
   - 私**は**田中です。 "As for me, I'm Tanaka." (introducing yourself)
   - 私**が**田中です。 "*I'm* the one who's Tanaka." (answering "Who is Tanaka?")
2. **Question words take が, never は.** だれ**が**来ましたか。 "Who came?" The answer keeps が: 田中さん**が**来ました。
3. **は adds contrast.** 肉**は**食べますが、魚**は**食べません。 "Meat I eat, but fish I don't."

Also: inside a relative or subordinate clause, the subject normally takes が, not は: 私**が**作ったケーキ "the cake I made".

And verbs of existence, ability and liking take が for the thing: 犬**が**好きです "I like dogs", 日本語**が**分かります "I understand Japanese".

## に vs で (both can be "at" or "in")

- **に**: where something **is**, or where something **goes**. 部屋**に**猫がいる。 "There's a cat in the room." / 学校**に**行く。 "go to school"
- **で**: where an action **happens**. 部屋**で**勉強する。 "study in the room"

Same place, different verb type: いる/ある (existence) take に; activity verbs take で.

で also marks means or tool: バス**で**行く "go by bus", 箸**で**食べる "eat with chopsticks".

に also marks specific times: 7時**に**起きる "get up at 7". Relative times like 今日 or 明日 normally take no particle.

## に vs へ

Both mark destination. へ stresses direction ("toward"); に stresses arrival ("to"). In everyday speech they are often interchangeable for movement verbs, but only に works for existence and time.

## Pronunciation notes

- は is pronounced **wa** when it's the particle, though it is ha elsewhere.
- へ is pronounced **e** as a particle.
- を is pronounced **o**.

## Common mistakes

| Wrong | Right | Why |
|---|---|---|
| だれは来ましたか。 | だれ**が**来ましたか。 | question words take が |
| 図書館に勉強する。 | 図書館**で**勉強する。 | action location takes で |
| 部屋で猫がいる。 | 部屋**に**猫がいる。 | existence location takes に |
| 私も**は**行く。 | 私**も**行く。 | も replaces は |
